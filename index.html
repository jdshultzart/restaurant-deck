How A Project Runs11 / 12
Process

How A
Project Runs

We work directly with owners, operators, architects and designers, so the finished install feels built in, not applied.

1Walkthrough. We walk the property, or review plans, and pick the surfaces with the most impact.
2Direction. We agree on the artistic direction with ownership and the design team.
3Design. Original artwork sized to your exact surfaces, with proofs for approval.
4Production. Materials finished for real use, including daily service and cleaning.
5Installation. Installed around your operating hours, with minimal disruption.
6Refresh. Seasonal or annual updates for properties that want the space to keep evolving.
