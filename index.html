How A Project Runs16 / 18
Process

How A Project Runs

Every project starts with collaboration. We work directly with owners, architects, interior designers, developers and operators so the finished install feels intentional and permanent, not applied.

1Walkthrough. We visit the property, or review plans and renderings, and identify the surfaces with the most impact.
2Direction. We agree on the artistic direction with your ownership, architect and design team.
3Design. I create original artwork sized and built for those exact surfaces, with proofs for approval.
4Production. Materials are produced and finished for the environment, including daily service and cleaning.
5Installation. Installed on your schedule, around your operating hours, with minimal disruption.
6Refresh. Seasonal or annual updates available for properties that want the space to keep evolving.
